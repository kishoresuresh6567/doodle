A MOMENT OF PLAY

A little good energy.

Choose an evil eye, dream catcher, pink pig, chillies and lemon, or a nazar doll for your moment of play. Pull the stretchy thread and release to watch your charm bounce and swing.

Grab the charm, pull it in any direction, and let go. Try a gentle swing or lift it up to watch the thread fall slack.

Keyboard: focus the charm with Tab, use arrow keys to swing, and Space to pause. Press R to reset.
